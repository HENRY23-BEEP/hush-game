HOW TO SURVIVE

Something lives in the lower levels of the Meridian Facility. It has no eyes. It hunts by sound alone. Find the fuses on each floor, power the service lift and get out.

W A S D Move (arrow keys also work)
MOUSE Look around (click the screen to capture the mouse)
SHIFT Sprint. Fast, but very loud.
C Crouch. Slow, but almost silent.
LEFT CLICK / Q Throw a bottle. Where it shatters, it will go.
E Use the lift
F Flashlight. It can't see your light, but your batteries won't save you.
ESC / P Pause

Watch your NOISE meter. Standing still makes no sound. Listen for the clicking: it gets faster when it's close. On a phone, use the left side to move, drag the right side to look.
